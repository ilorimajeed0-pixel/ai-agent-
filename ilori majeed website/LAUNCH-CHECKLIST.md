Send these details before the website goes live.

## Required content

- [ ] Hero image — ideally 1800 × 1100, dark or blue-toned.
- [ ] Three service-card images: AI Voice Agents, Automation Workflows, and Chatbot/CRM Integrations.
- [ ] Booking URL from Calendly, Cal.com, or another booking service.
- [ ] City and country.
- [ ] LinkedIn, X, YouTube, and Instagram URLs. Links that are not needed can be removed.
- [ ] Privacy Policy page or URL.
- [ ] Two additional project names, descriptions, and images, or approval to remove those placeholders.
- [ ] Verified result numbers for Projects, Businesses Supported, Hours Saved Weekly, and Client Satisfaction.
- [ ] Three real client testimonials with name, role, and company, or approval to remove the section temporarily.
- [ ] Confirm or replace the “10+ hours saved weekly” highlight.
- [ ] Confirm or replace the four skill percentages.
- [ ] Approve the About text.

## SEO and sharing
